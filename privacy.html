Privacy Policy

BillParse · Effective date: November 5, 2026 · Last updated: November 5, 2026

BillParse (“we”, “us”, or “the App”) helps you photograph or upload receipts, extract merchant, date, totals, and line items, review or edit that data on your device, and export it as a spreadsheet. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the BillParse mobile application distributed through Google Play and to related demo or web builds that provide the same features. By using BillParse, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Information we process

BillParse is designed to work without creating an account. Depending on how you use the App, we may process:

  • Receipt images. Photos you take with the camera or images you select from your photo library so the App can read a receipt.
  • Extracted receipt data. Merchant name, date, totals, line items, and any edits you make. This data is stored locally on your device for history, editing, copying, and export.
  • App preferences and entitlements. Local values such as remaining free scans, premium unlock status (monthly or lifetime), and your receipt history.
  • Purchase information. If you buy a subscription or lifetime unlock, payment is handled by Google Play. We receive purchase or restore results needed to unlock features; we do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Advertising interactions. If you choose to watch a rewarded ad for a free scan or a one-time export, advertising partners may process device and ad-related identifiers as described in their policies.
  • Clipboard content you choose to copy. If you tap Copy, receipt text is placed on your device clipboard.
  • Exported files you create. Spreadsheet files you export are generated on your device and shared through the system share sheet or downloaded locally. We do not host those files for you.

BillParse does not ask for your name, email address, or password to use core features. If you contact us for support, we will use the contact details you provide only to respond.

3. How we use information

  • To recognize receipt content and show editable results
  • To keep a local history of receipts you scanned or edited
  • To manage free scans, premium unlocks, restore purchases, and one-time ad rewards
  • To let you copy receipt text or export spreadsheets
  • To operate, secure, and improve the App
  • To comply with law or respond to valid legal requests

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Camera and photo library

BillParse requests camera access only to photograph receipts, and photo library access only so you can choose one or more receipt images (up to the App’s batch limit). Images are used for recognition and optional local history. You can deny or revoke these permissions in your device settings; without them, the related features will not work.

5. Receipt recognition and third-party AI

When receipt recognition is enabled, selected receipt images (and the data needed to process them) are sent to OpenAI’s API so the App can extract visible receipt text into structured fields. OpenAI processes that content according to its own terms and privacy policy: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.

Please avoid uploading receipts that contain sensitive personal information you do not want processed for recognition. If recognition is not configured, the App may show a sample result instead of sending an image.

6. Local storage

Credits, premium status, and receipt history are stored on your device (for example with on-device storage such as AsyncStorage). Uninstalling the App, clearing app data, or using a different device may remove this information. Cached camera or photo URIs may also stop working after storage is cleared.

7. Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play. Google’s privacy practices apply to payment processing. BillParse uses purchase and restore signals from the store to unlock unlimited scans and spreadsheet export. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Google Play account settings.

8. Advertising

BillParse may show rewarded ads through Google AdMob when you choose options such as “watch an ad for 1 free scan” or “watch an ad to export once.” Ad partners may collect device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, and interaction data to deliver and measure ads. See Google’s policies for details: https://policies.google.com/privacy and AdMob & privacy.

9. Sharing of information

We share information only as needed to provide the App:

  • OpenAI, for receipt recognition when that feature is enabled
  • Google Play, for billing and purchase restoration
  • Google AdMob / Google advertising services, when you watch rewarded ads
  • Service providers who help us host this privacy policy or operate infrastructure, if any
  • Authorities, when required by law

We do not share your receipt history with other users. Exported files leave the App only when you choose to share or save them.

10. Data retention

Receipt images used for a recognition request are processed to produce a result and are not kept by BillParse as an online account archive, because BillParse does not provide a user account cloud backup. Local history remains on your device until you delete an item, clear app data, or uninstall the App. Purchase records remain subject to Google Play’s retention practices. Advertising partners retain data according to their own policies.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the App. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. You are responsible for controlling physical access to your device and for how you share exported spreadsheets.

12. Children’s privacy

BillParse is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

13. International processing

Third-party services used by BillParse, including OpenAI and Google, may process data in the United States or other countries. Those countries may have different data-protection laws from your own.

14. Your choices

  • Deny or revoke camera and photo library permissions in device settings
  • Delete individual receipts from History in the App
  • Clear app storage or uninstall BillParse to remove local data
  • Skip rewarded ads and use purchases instead, or the reverse
  • Manage subscriptions and restore purchases through Google Play
  • Use platform ad or privacy settings available on your device where offered

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of BillParse after an update means you accept the revised policy. Material changes may also be noted on the App’s store listing when appropriate.
